The shop

Kopou is a small family florist on the Chowkidinghee road. Two counters, one cool room, and a bench by the window where everything gets tied.

The name is the kopou phool — the foxtail orchid you tuck into your hair at Bihu. It is the first flower we learned to handle, and it still sets the standard for the rest: nothing forced, nothing too perfect, just what is good that week.

We start early. The flowers come in before the heat does, mostly from growers up the valley, and whatever will not last the day does not make it to the shelf. Everything else is tied to order — we would rather build a bouquet around what you actually need than hand you something that has been sitting since Tuesday.

There is no online payment here, and no warehouse behind the website. When you send an order through this site, it reaches us the same way a phone call would: we read it, check the flowers, and message you back on WhatsApp to agree the address and the time. Then you pay the delivery person, in cash or by UPI.

✦

Every order is confirmed personally, by phone or on WhatsApp, before it leaves the shop. If you need it today, order before 4:00 PM and message us — we will tell you honestly whether we can make it.
